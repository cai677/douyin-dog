# Six Shot Workflow

Use these prompts as the baseline for `version-01-bichon`. For other versions, preserve shot timing, story function, and file naming while replacing pet and germ design variables.

## Timing

```text
shot-01  0.0 to 1.4 seconds
shot-02  1.4 to 3.0 seconds
shot-03  3.0 to 4.7 seconds
shot-04  4.7 to 6.4 seconds
shot-05  6.4 to 8.1 seconds
shot-06  8.1 to 10.8 seconds
```

## shot-01 Eye Micro World

Purpose: Establish that the viewer is inside the pet eye and tear-stain micro-world.

Dialogue: none.

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, cute polished 3D pet product commercial style, extreme close-up of a fluffy white bichon frise dog's single big glossy eye, the eye and lower tear-stain area fill most of the frame, tiny cartoon germ characters wandering around the brownish tear-stain area under the eye, a small messy tear-stain base like a funny villain hideout, warm indoor light, clean bright colors, cute not scary, family friendly, no text, no watermark.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 1.4-second opening shot. Keep the camera in an extreme close-up of the bichon's eye. Tiny cute germ characters wander around the tear-stain base, chatting and acting relaxed. Add subtle eye sparkle, tiny character movements, and a slow gentle camera push-in. Cute comedic commercial style, no text.
```

Redo if the viewer cannot immediately identify the eye, tear stain, and germ base.

## shot-02 Cotton Pad Approaches

Dialogue:

```text
角色1：哥，擦眼睛的来了！
```

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, same bichon eye close-up, cute 3D pet commercial style. A soft white cotton pad held by a human hand enters from the right edge, approaching the bichon's eye. A nervous green germ soldier near the lower eye corner points at the cotton pad with a shocked face. Other tiny germs turn their heads. Keep the eye large and central, tear-stain area visible, bright clean warm lighting, no text.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 1.6-second shot. The cotton pad slowly approaches from the right side. The nervous germ soldier suddenly notices it, jumps slightly, points toward it, and panics. Other germs look over. Keep the motion clear and comedic, with a slight camera shake or quick push-in for surprise. No text.
```

Redo if the cotton pad is unclear, the germ does not notice it, or the action is too chaotic.

## shot-03 Germ Boss Dismisses Wiping

Dialogue:

```text
角色2：没事，擦表面，对我们没用。
```

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, extreme close-up of bichon eye and tear-stain base. A purple cartoon germ boss with a tiny crown sits confidently on the tear-stain base like a villain leader, waving dismissively. The nervous germ soldier stands beside him, still worried. Cotton pad is visible near the right edge but not touching the germs. Cute polished 3D animation style, expressive faces, warm bright pet ad look, no text.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 1.7-second shot. The germ boss casually waves one hand dismissively and smirks, acting confident and arrogant. The nervous germ soldier looks back and forth between the boss and the cotton pad. The cotton pad lightly wipes the surface area nearby, but the germ base remains. Comedic timing, bright clean style, no text.
```

Redo if the boss does not look confident or the remaining germ base is unclear after wiping.

## shot-04 Eye Drops Appear

Dialogue:

```text
角色1：什么东西过来了？
```

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, same bichon eye close-up. The cotton pad has just passed by the tear-stain area. Above the eye, the tip of an eye-drop bottle enters from the top edge, releasing one clear transparent droplet. The nervous germ soldier looks upward in shock. The germ boss is still confident but beginning to notice something. Cute 3D pet commercial style, clean transparent droplet, no product label, no text, no watermark.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 1.7-second shot. The cotton pad moves slightly away. The eye-drop bottle tip appears from the top edge and a single clear droplet forms slowly. The nervous germ soldier looks up and points at the droplet in fear. The droplet grows and begins to fall gently. Add a slight dramatic pause and bright highlight on the droplet. No text.
```

Redo if the bottle or droplet is unclear, or if it visually merges with the cotton pad action.

## shot-05 Warning Reversal

Dialogue:

```text

```

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, bichon eye close-up, the clear eye-drop droplet is now descending above the tear-stain base. Comedic red warning light glows across the tiny germ hideout. The purple germ boss looks up with a terrified expression, crown tilted, mouth open. Other germs panic and run around. Cute 3D animated commercial style, dramatic but funny, no horror, no text.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 1.7-second shot. A soft red warning light flashes comedically over the germ base. The germ boss looks up, his confident face changes into panic, his crown tilts, and he steps backward. Tiny germs run in circles. The clear droplet descends closer but has not landed yet. Exaggerated facial expressions, funny timing, no text.
```

Redo if there is no obvious expression change from confidence to panic.

## shot-06 Germ Base Washed Away

Dialogue:

```text
角色1+2+3：身体好像被掏空，我们老窝都让人端了！
```

End caption:

```text
眼屎多、泪痕重，别只擦表面
```

Keyframe prompt:

```text
9:16 vertical, extreme close-up of the bichon's eye and tear-stain area. A clear gentle eye-drop droplet lands near the lower eye corner, creating a clean transparent splash wave that washes through the tiny tear-stain germ base. Germ characters are lifted and tumbling away in a funny exaggerated way. The bichon's eye looks glossy and clean, cute polished 3D pet commercial style, bright and family friendly, no scary medical imagery, no text.
```

Video prompt:

```text
Animate this as a 2.7-second shot. The clear droplet lands softly near the tear-stain area and spreads like a clean transparent wave. The tiny germ base breaks apart in a comedic way. Germ characters tumble, spin, and get washed away while making exaggerated panic expressions. By the end, the eye area looks brighter and cleaner, with the bichon's eye sparkling gently. Keep everything cute, not scary. No generated text.
```

Redo if the cleaning result is unclear or the image becomes scary, gross, or medicalized.
